[01] How we test

From your job to a scorecard.

We test the robot AI on your real job: first thousands of times in simulation, then on a real robot. You get one page your buyers can trust, and the method behind it.

  1. Define the jobYou tell us the task, the items and what counts as success.01
  2. SimulateThousands of runs, changing the light, the positions and the packaging.02
  3. Test on a real robotA smaller set of real trials on your robot, or a matching one.03
  4. ScorecardHow often it succeeds, how fast, how often a person steps in, and why it fails.04
  5. Fix it, then retestEvery update gets a fresh score, so your buyers can see what changed.05
[02] What we check

Six things we measure on every robot.

Every test ends with the same six numbers, so a buyer can compare any robot AI fairly. Each one comes with the videos and the method behind it.

  1. 01Does it do the job?Success rate

    Out of every 100 tries, how many it gets right. We give an honest range, because 1,000 tries tell you more than 10.

    Example: 94 out of 100, likely 92–95
  2. 02Why does it fail?Failure types

    Every failure is sorted: it dropped something, picked the wrong thing, bumped into something or froze. Each one comes with a video clip.

    Example: fails most on shiny packets
  3. 03How often does a person have to help?Step-ins per hour

    Every time someone walks over to help, or takes control from a distance, we count it.

    Example: 4 times an hour
  4. 04Is it fast enough?Speed

    The time each task takes, next to a person doing the same job.

    Example: 9 seconds per item; a person takes 6
  5. 05Does it cope with change?Robustness

    We change the light, the positions, the items and the wording of the instruction, and measure how much the score drops.

    Example: in bright light, 94 drops to 71
  6. 06Is it safe around people?Near misses and bumps

    How close it comes to people, and everything it bumps into. We report what happened. We don't certify.

    Example: 0 near misses, 2 bumps

[04] Our rules

Independence is the product.

A test is only worth something if nobody can lean on the result. These rules don't bend for anyone, including paying customers.

01Scores never flatter.We report what happened, even when the customer won't like it. Our fee is fixed and never depends on the score.
02We don't make robot AI.We don't build it, sell it or favour any of it. A referee doesn't play in the match.
03Your data stays yours.Your videos and logs stay private. We publish only the results you agree to.
04We publish the method.Anyone can rerun our tests and check our numbers.
